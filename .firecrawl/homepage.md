## Produk

### 1. Cilembu Sweet Potato (Signature Honey Variety)
- Tumbuh di volcanic highlands Cilembu, West Java
- Rasa: natural caramel-like syrup ketika dipanggang ("honey sweet potato")
- Low fiber, dense texture, legendary sweetness
- Premium Grade A export

### 2. Purple Sweet Potato (Antioxidant Powerhouse)
- Superfood untuk health-conscious markets
- Tinggi antioksidan, mildly sweet
- Ideal untuk flour, snacks, bakery
- Deep violet, anthocyanin-rich flesh
- Ditanam di tropical lowlands Indonesia

### 3. Murasaki Sweet Potato (Japanese Heritage Cultivar)
- Kulit reddish-purple, interior creamy white
- Rasa: chestnut-like, nutty sweetness
- Premium untuk retail produce & gourmet foodservice
- Japanese cultivar yang kini tumbuh di Indonesia
- Long shelf stability, retail-ready uniformity
